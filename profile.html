Standard Information
What do these mean?

Pureblood — individuals who strictly have Elven-human or magickal human blood.

Half-blooded — individuals who have either Elven-human or magickal human blood, mixed with human blood.

Non-Magickal-Born — individuals who are born from humans with no magickal abilities.

Maladroit — half-blooded individuals who were born without any magickal abilities.

Corrupt-blooded — individuals who are vampires or half vampires.

What do these mean?

Elves (unplayable — shown for reference only) — the founders of The Society of Magickal Folk, considered so beautiful they could charm other magical beings, and capable of strong ancient magic passed down through generations.

Elven-Human — the 'true' Pure-blood witches: half-Elven, half-Magickal Human. They have the rare potential to inherit ancient magic, which can be life-threatening if handled incorrectly.

Magickal Human — those who possess a very similar biology to elves.

Human — non-magickal and magickal folk who don't share similar biology to elves, from ordinary backgrounds.

Kthenosthrope — those who are half-human, half-beast, e.g. Centaurs, Ichthyocentaurs, Satyrs, Merfolk, Cynocephaly.

Half-Breed — those who are Kthenosthrope or any other magical race, like Corrupt-blooded races such as a vampire, mixed with Elven-human or human.

Vampire — usually pale and gaunt; dark, mortal, magickal beings associated with the living dead. They're revenants — former humans reanimated from corrupt magick — and can be born or turned. They have sharp fangs, are nocturnal due to light sensitivity, and while their diet is normal, they must consume blood to sustain their reanimated state and their sanity. A vampire starved of blood returns to a lifeless state and cannot be reanimated again.

Earliest possible birth year is 1944.

About wand components

Cores — rare cores, including Basilisk Fang, Griffin Feather and Wyvern Spike, are reserved for professors and high-ranking or combat-related Wizarding Gov. U.K. staff. If your character doesn't fall under these categories, you must pick a common core.

Adaptability describes how loyal a wand is to its first wielder, from least to most loyal: Supple, Bendy, Amenable, Finicky, Obstinate.
