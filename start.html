Start Here

how to create your character and begin playing at the Hogarth Institute

A quick rundown of what to do before you jump in.

01
Join the Discord
The Hogarth community, announcements, and support all happen on Discord. Join the server if you haven't already.
Join the Discord
02
Read the Encyclopedia
Get familiar with the world, its rules, and its classes before you build a character around them.
Read the Encyclopedia
03
Create Your Character
Head to your Profile and sign in with Discord. Once you're in, you can register a character with a name, age, house or affiliation, and a short bio. How many characters you can make depends on your server rank.
Go to Profile
04
Begin Playing
Once your character is registered, connect to the server and jump in. Any questions along the way — ask in Discord.
